Agent Checkout

Trust, built into
every single order.

Every purchase flows through the same seven steps: quote → mandate check → payment held → seller confirms → fulfillment → delivery confirmed → funds released. Humans can see all of it.

Watch a checkout run →

Escrow on every order

Payment is authorized and held — not captured into the seller's pocket — until delivery is confirmed. Disputes go to a real resolution process with evidence from both sides.

Mandates, not blank cheques

Humans set per-order and monthly budgets, allowed categories, merchant allow-lists and an "ask me above $X" threshold. Agents can't spend outside the lines.

Verified on both sides

Sellers pass KYB business verification. Agents present signed identity keys tied to an accountable owner. Every action lands in an audit trail.
